# Loyalty Program

The Bloom & Brew loyalty program, "The Regulars," rewards our most dedicated customers and drives repeat visits. It launched 8 months ago and has exceeded all expectations.

## Program Structure

### Tiers

| Tier | Requirement | Benefits |
|------|------------|----------|
| Seedling | Sign up | Every 10th coffee free, birthday bouquet ($25 value) |
| Sprout | $500 lifetime spend | 10% off arrangements, early access to workshops |
| In Full Bloom | $1,500 lifetime spend | 15% off all products, free monthly coffee bag, VIP event invites |

## Enrollment

- 450 active members (see [[Email Marketing]] VIP segment)
- Sign up via: POS at register, website, QR code in-store
- Enrollment has been boosted by [[Social Media Strategy]] promotions and [[Seasonal Campaigns]]

## Points System

- $1 spent = 1 point
- 100 points = $5 reward
- Bonus point events during [[Seasonal Campaigns]] (double points days)
- Workshop attendance = 25 bonus points

## Program Performance

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Enrollment rate | 15% of new customers |
| Active rate | 72% |
| Avg spend increase | +35% vs non-members |
| Visit frequency | 2.8x more than non-members |

## Technology

Currently tracked via our POS system and a supplementary spreadsheet. The [[Tech Stack and Tools]] evaluation includes a potential upgrade to a dedicated loyalty platform. Budget allocated in [[Budget Planning]].

## Integration

- [[Email Marketing]]: Loyalty members get exclusive content and early access
- [[Customer Feedback Program]]: Loyalty members are surveyed separately for deeper insights
- [[Partnership Opportunities]]: Partner discounts stack with loyalty benefits
- [[Product Catalog]]: Loyalty-exclusive seasonal items
- [[Pricing Strategy]]: Loyalty discounts factored into margin calculations

## Growth Goals

Per the [[Growth Roadmap]], we aim for 800 loyalty members within the next year. Strategies include referral bonuses, workshop enrollment incentives, and [[Social Media Strategy]] giveaways. Tracked at [[Quarterly Business Review]].
