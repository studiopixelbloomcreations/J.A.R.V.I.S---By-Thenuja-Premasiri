# Customer Feedback Program

Listening to our customers is how Bloom & Brew stays relevant and continues to improve. Our feedback program captures voice-of-customer data across multiple touchpoints.

## Feedback Channels

### In-Store
- Comment cards at the register (refreshed weekly)
- QR code on receipts linking to a short survey
- Direct conversation — our [[Team Roster]] is trained to ask "How was everything?"

### Digital
- Google Reviews (currently 4.7 stars, 380+ reviews)
- Yelp (4.5 stars, 210+ reviews)
- Instagram DMs and comments
- [[Email Marketing]] reply-based feedback

### Structured
- Post-workshop survey (sent within 24 hours)
- [[Loyalty Program]] member quarterly survey
- Net Promoter Score survey (sent quarterly to full [[Email Marketing]] list)

## Feedback Loop

1. All feedback is logged in our tracking spreadsheet (see [[Tech Stack and Tools]])
2. Urgent issues (rating ≤ 2) are addressed within 24 hours by Jordan
3. Weekly feedback summary discussed at [[Daily Standup Process]] on Mondays
4. Monthly trends compiled for [[Quarterly Business Review]]
5. Product and service changes driven by feedback are noted in [[Product Catalog]]

## Recent Actions from Feedback

- Added oat milk as default alt-milk option (top request)
- Extended Saturday hours to 9 PM (requested by workshop attendees)
- Created a "quiet corner" seating area (customers wanted a calm space)
- Introduced smaller arrangement sizes at lower price points (see [[Pricing Strategy]])

## Review Response Strategy

All Google and Yelp reviews get a response within 48 hours. Positive reviews get a genuine thank you. Negative reviews get a professional response and an offline follow-up. Our [[Social Media Strategy]] and [[Brand Guidelines]] govern the tone of all public responses.
