# Training and Onboarding

A smooth onboarding experience sets new team members up for success at Bloom & Brew. Given our dual-concept studio, training is more involved than a typical café or flower shop.

## Onboarding Timeline

### Week 1: Orientation
- Studio tour and introduction to [[Team Roster]]
- Review [[Brand Guidelines]] and company values
- Safety training and [[Facilities Maintenance]] basics
- Shadow shifts with their department lead
- Set up on all systems (see [[Tech Stack and Tools]])

### Week 2: Core Skills
- **Coffee track:** Espresso basics, milk steaming, drink recipes from [[Product Catalog]]
- **Floral track:** Flower care, basic arrangements, cooler management
- [[Inventory Management]] procedures
- POS system training

### Week 3: Independence
- Solo shifts with mentor nearby
- Introduction to [[Shift Scheduling]] and swap procedures
- Begin participating in [[Daily Standup Process]]
- Start cross-training intro (coffee staff learn basic floral, vice versa)

### Week 4: Full Integration
- Independent shifts
- Customer service excellence training
- [[Customer Feedback Program]] overview
- Introduction to [[Loyalty Program]] procedures
- 30-day check-in with manager

## Ongoing Development

- Monthly skill-building sessions (new drink recipes, arrangement techniques)
- Annual SCA certification opportunity for baristas (budgeted in [[Budget Planning]])
- Floral design certifications encouraged
- Cross-training program: 2 hours/week learning the other department
- Performance reviews every 6 months, discussed at [[Quarterly Business Review]]

## Training Materials

All training materials are stored in our shared Drive. The [[Tech Stack and Tools]] document has access links. We update materials seasonally to reflect new [[Product Catalog]] items and [[Seasonal Campaigns]] preparation needs.

## Knowledge Sharing

We encourage the team to share tips in our group chat and during [[Daily Standup Process]]. Sarah and Aisha run monthly "teach me something" sessions where they share advanced techniques with the team.
