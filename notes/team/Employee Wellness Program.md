# Employee Wellness Program

At Bloom & Brew, we believe that happy, healthy team members create the best experiences for our customers. Our wellness program is modest but meaningful.

## Core Benefits

- **Health Stipend:** $100/month health and wellness stipend (gym, therapy, etc.)
- **Free Drinks:** Unlimited coffee and tea during shifts
- **Staff Discount:** 50% off all products (see [[Pricing Strategy]])
- **Flexible Scheduling:** We accommodate school schedules, second jobs, and life events through [[Shift Scheduling]]
- **Mental Health Days:** 3 additional days per year, no questions asked

## PTO Policy

- Full-time: 10 days PTO + 3 mental health days + holidays
- Part-time: 5 days PTO + 2 mental health days + holidays
- PTO requests go through [[Shift Scheduling]] process
- Blackout dates around major [[Seasonal Campaigns]] (Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, holidays)

## Team Building

- Monthly team outings (budgeted in [[Budget Planning]])
- Quarterly team dinner after [[Quarterly Business Review]]
- Annual summer picnic
- [[Daily Standup Process]] "kudos" moment for peer recognition

## Professional Development

- See [[Training and Onboarding]] for certification opportunities
- Conference attendance for leads (one per year)
- Book club (current read: "Setting the Table" by Danny Meyer)

## Wellness Check-ins

- Monthly anonymous pulse survey (3 questions)
- Quarterly 1:1 with direct manager
- Annual comprehensive satisfaction survey
- Results discussed at [[Quarterly Business Review]] and influence [[Growth Roadmap]] people strategy

## Open Door Policy

Maya and Jordan maintain an open door policy. Any [[Team Roster]] member can schedule time to discuss concerns, ideas, or career goals. We also monitor the [[Customer Feedback Program]] for any mentions of specific team members to address both praise and concerns.

## Future Plans

As we grow per the [[Growth Roadmap]], we plan to add dental insurance and increase the wellness stipend. This is tracked in [[Budget Planning]] as a future line item.
