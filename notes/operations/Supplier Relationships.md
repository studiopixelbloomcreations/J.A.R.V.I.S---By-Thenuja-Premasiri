# Supplier Relationships

Strong supplier relationships are a competitive advantage for Bloom & Brew. We prioritize local, sustainable, and ethical sourcing across both our coffee and floral lines.

## Coffee Suppliers

### Altitude Roasters Cooperative (Primary)
- Single-origin beans from Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala
- Fair trade certified, direct trade relationships
- Delivery: Every other Monday
- Payment terms: Net 30
- Contact: Miguel Santos, miguel@altitudecoop.com

### Local Dairy — Meadowbrook Farms
- Whole milk, oat milk, cream
- Delivery: Mon/Wed/Fri before 6 AM
- Ties into our [[Brand Guidelines]] commitment to local sourcing

## Floral Suppliers

### Valley Bloom Wholesale
- Primary source for roses, lilies, seasonal stems
- Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday deliveries
- We get first pick before 5 AM — early [[Shift Scheduling]] matters here

### Dried & Preserved — Everlast Botanicals
- Dried flowers, preserved eucalyptus, craft supplies for workshops
- Monthly bulk orders, 15% volume discount above $500

## Relationship Management

- Quarterly check-ins with all primary suppliers
- Annual review of terms during [[Quarterly Business Review]]
- We feature supplier stories in our [[Social Media Strategy]] content
- New supplier evaluation criteria are documented in the [[Pricing Strategy]] to ensure margin targets

## Backup Suppliers

We maintain backup relationships for every critical category. Supply chain disruptions during previous years taught us this lesson. Details in [[Inventory Management]].
