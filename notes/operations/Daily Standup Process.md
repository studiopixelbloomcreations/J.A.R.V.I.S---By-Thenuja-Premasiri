# Daily Standup Process

Our daily standup meetings are the heartbeat of Bloom & Brew, our artisan coffee and floral design studio. Every morning at 8:45 AM, before the shop opens, the entire [[Team Roster]] gathers near the espresso bar for a quick 10-minute check-in.

## Format

Each team member answers three questions:
1. What did you accomplish yesterday?
2. What are you working on today?
3. Any blockers?

## Key Rules

- Keep it under 10 minutes — if a topic needs deeper discussion, schedule a separate huddle
- Stand up! No sitting allowed — it keeps energy high
- The [[Shift Scheduling]] board should be updated before standup
- Any issues with [[Inventory Management]] should be flagged here

## Notes

We started this practice after our Q2 review showed communication gaps between the floral and coffee sides of the business. Since implementing standups, our [[Customer Feedback Program]] scores have improved by 18%. The [[Quarterly Business Review]] now tracks standup attendance as a KPI.

Sarah from the floral team suggested adding a "kudos" moment at the end where someone can shout out a teammate. This has been great for morale and ties into our [[Employee Wellness Program]].
