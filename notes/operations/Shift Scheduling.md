# Shift Scheduling

Managing shifts at Bloom & Brew requires balancing two distinct skill sets: baristas and floral designers. Some team members are cross-trained, which gives us flexibility.

## Shift Structure

| Shift | Time | Min Staff |
|-------|------|-----------|
| Opening | 6:00 AM - 2:00 PM | 3 (2 coffee, 1 floral) |
| Closing | 12:00 PM - 8:00 PM | 3 (2 coffee, 1 floral) |
| Weekend | 7:00 AM - 9:00 PM | 5 (3 coffee, 2 floral) |

## Scheduling Rules

- Schedule published every Thursday for the following week
- Swap requests must be submitted 48 hours in advance
- At least one [[Team Roster]] member with keyholder access on every shift
- During peak seasons (Valentine's, Mother's Day), all hands on deck — see [[Seasonal Campaigns]]

## Tools

We currently use a shared Google Sheet, but we're evaluating dedicated scheduling software as part of our [[Tech Stack and Tools]] review. The [[Budget Planning]] for next quarter includes a line item for this.

## Time Off

All PTO requests go through the process outlined in the [[Employee Wellness Program]]. We require two weeks' notice for time off during non-peak periods and blackout dates around major holidays.

## Coverage

If someone calls out, the shift lead follows the call tree in the [[Team Roster]] document. Cross-trained staff from [[Training and Onboarding]] are our first backup option.
