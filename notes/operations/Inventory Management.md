# Inventory Management

Effective inventory management is critical for Bloom & Brew since we deal with two highly perishable product categories: fresh flowers and specialty coffee beans.

## Coffee Inventory

- Green beans are ordered bi-weekly from our [[Supplier Relationships]] partners
- Roasted beans have a 14-day freshness window
- We maintain a minimum stock of 20 lbs per variety
- Reorder triggers are managed through our spreadsheet system (see [[Tech Stack and Tools]])

## Floral Inventory

- Fresh flowers are sourced three times a week from local growers and the wholesale market
- Seasonal availability drives our [[Product Catalog]] offerings
- Waste tracking is essential — we aim for under 8% spoilage
- Dried flower supplies for our workshop series are ordered monthly

## Storage

The back room is split into zones: cold storage (flowers, 34-38°F), cool storage (beans, 60-65°F), and dry storage (packaging, retail items). The [[Facilities Maintenance]] schedule includes daily temperature checks.

## Counting

Full inventory counts happen every Sunday evening after close. The [[Shift Scheduling]] always assigns two people for this task. Discrepancies over $50 are reported in the [[Quarterly Business Review]].

## Integration with Sales

Our [[Revenue Streams]] analysis shows that inventory turns directly impact margin. The [[Pricing Strategy]] document outlines our markup targets per category.
