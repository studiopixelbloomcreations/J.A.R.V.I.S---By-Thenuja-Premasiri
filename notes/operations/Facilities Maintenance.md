# Facilities Maintenance

Our space at 412 Magnolia Avenue is a converted 1920s brick warehouse — beautiful, but it needs consistent upkeep. Maintaining the aesthetic is key to our [[Brand Guidelines]] and customer experience.

## Daily Tasks

- [ ] Sweep and mop all floors (hardwood in café, tile in workshop)
- [ ] Clean espresso machines and grinders (see [[Product Catalog]] for equipment list)
- [ ] Wipe down all display surfaces and the floral cooler glass
- [ ] Empty compost bins (coffee grounds + floral waste go to our partner garden)
- [ ] Check cold storage temperatures and log in [[Inventory Management]] sheet

## Weekly Tasks

- Deep clean restrooms
- Polish brass fixtures and vintage signage
- Water the living wall installation
- Clean exterior windows and sweep the patio seating area

## Monthly Tasks

- HVAC filter replacement
- Pest control inspection
- Grease trap cleaning
- Review [[Budget Planning]] for any upcoming capital maintenance needs

## Vendor Contacts

All maintenance vendor information is stored in the [[Supplier Relationships]] file. Our landlord handles structural issues but we're responsible for interior maintenance per our lease terms.

## Renovation Plans

We're planning a patio expansion for Q2 next year. This ties into our [[Growth Roadmap]] and the [[Revenue Streams]] goal of adding outdoor event hosting. The [[Quarterly Business Review]] will include construction timeline updates.
