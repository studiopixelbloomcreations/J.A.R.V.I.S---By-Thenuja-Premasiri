# Partnership Opportunities

Strategic partnerships extend Bloom & Brew's reach and create new [[Revenue Streams]] without significant capital investment.

## Active Partnerships

### The Magnolia Inn (Boutique Hotel, 2 blocks away)
- We supply daily lobby arrangements and in-room welcome bouquets
- Their guests receive 10% discount at our studio
- Joint promotion in their guest welcome packet
- Revenue: ~$1,200/month

### Oakwood Community Center
- Monthly floral arrangement workshop series
- They provide the space, we provide materials and instruction
- Split ticket revenue 70/30 (us/them)
- Great for [[Email Marketing]] list growth

### Local Wedding Planners Network
- Preferred vendor status with 4 local wedding planners
- Commission: 8% referral fee on bookings over $500
- [[Product Catalog]] includes dedicated wedding packages

All partnerships must adhere to our [[Brand Guidelines]]. Co-branded materials require approval from our marketing lead. Partner stories are featured in our [[Social Media Strategy]] content calendar.

We evaluate partnerships quarterly in the [[Quarterly Business Review]] using: revenue generated, brand alignment, customer acquisition cost, and operational burden. [[Budget Planning]] tracks partnership-related expenses separately.
