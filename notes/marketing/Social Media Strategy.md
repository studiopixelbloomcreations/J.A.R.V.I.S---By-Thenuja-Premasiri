# Social Media Strategy

Bloom & Brew's social media presence is our primary marketing engine. Our unique combination of coffee and flowers creates highly visual, shareable content that performs exceptionally well on Instagram and TikTok.

## Platforms & Posting Cadence

| Platform | Posts/Week | Content Type |
|----------|-----------|--------------|
| Instagram | 5-7 | Reels, carousel, stories |
| TikTok | 3-4 | Behind-the-scenes, tutorials |
| Facebook | 2-3 | Events, community posts |
| Pinterest | 5+ | Product pins, mood boards |

## Content Pillars

1. **Behind the Bloom** — Process videos showing floral arrangement creation
2. **Bean to Cup** — Coffee sourcing and brewing content (ties into [[Supplier Relationships]] stories)
3. **Studio Life** — Day-in-the-life of the [[Team Roster]] members
4. **Customer Spotlight** — UGC and testimonials from our [[Customer Feedback Program]]
5. **Seasonal Magic** — Content aligned with [[Seasonal Campaigns]]

## Engagement Strategy

- Respond to all comments within 2 hours during business hours
- Share user-generated content at least twice weekly
- Collaborate with local influencers monthly (see [[Partnership Opportunities]])
- Use our [[Brand Guidelines]] consistently across all platforms

## Metrics

We track follower growth, engagement rate, and conversion to store visits. Monthly reports feed into the [[Quarterly Business Review]]. Our [[Email Marketing]] list growth is partially driven by social media CTAs.

## Budget

Social media ad spend is allocated in [[Budget Planning]]. We typically boost our best-performing organic posts rather than running cold ads.
