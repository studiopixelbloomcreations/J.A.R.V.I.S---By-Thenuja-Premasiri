# Email Marketing

Email remains our highest-ROI marketing channel at Bloom & Brew, with an average open rate of 42% and a click-through rate of 8.3% — well above industry benchmarks.

## List Segments

| Segment | Size | Description |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Full List | ~4,200 | Everyone who has opted in |
| Coffee Lovers | ~2,800 | Primarily purchase coffee products |
| Flower Enthusiasts | ~1,900 | Buy arrangements or attend workshops |
| VIP / Loyalty | ~450 | Top 10% by spend, see [[Loyalty Program]] |
| Workshop Alumni | ~600 | Have attended at least one workshop |

## Email Calendar

- **Weekly:** "This Week at Bloom & Brew" — events, featured products, one piece of content
- **Monthly:** "The Bouquet" newsletter — deeper stories, [[Supplier Relationships]] features, team spotlights
- **Seasonal:** Campaign-specific emails tied to [[Seasonal Campaigns]]
- **Triggered:** Welcome series (3 emails), post-purchase thank you, birthday/anniversary

## Design Standards

All emails follow our [[Brand Guidelines]]. We use a consistent template with our green header, cream body, and terracotta CTAs.

## Growth Tactics

- In-store QR code sign-up (10% off first online order)
- [[Social Media Strategy]] cross-promotion
- Workshop registration requires email opt-in
- Referral program integration with [[Loyalty Program]]

## Analytics

Monthly email performance is reviewed in the [[Quarterly Business Review]]. We A/B test subject lines on every send to the full list.

## Tech

We use Mailchimp (free tier currently sufficient). This is tracked in [[Tech Stack and Tools]] and the [[Budget Planning]] includes a potential upgrade to paid tier.
