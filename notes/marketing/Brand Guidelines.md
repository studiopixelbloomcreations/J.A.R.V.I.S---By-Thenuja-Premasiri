# Brand Guidelines

Bloom & Brew's brand identity reflects the intersection of artisan coffee culture and botanical beauty. Every touchpoint should feel warm, organic, and slightly luxurious.

## Brand Essence

**Tagline:** "Where Coffee Meets Flowers"
**Mission:** To create a sensory sanctuary where the ritual of great coffee and the beauty of fresh flowers elevate everyday moments.

## Visual Identity

### Colors
- Primary: Deep Forest Green (#2D5016) and Warm Cream (#F5F0E8)
- Secondary: Terracotta (#C4703F), Dusty Rose (#C9929B), Coffee Brown (#6F4E37)
- Accent: Gold (#C5A55A) for premium elements

### Typography
- Headlines: Playfair Display (serif, elegant)
- Body: Inter (clean, readable)
- Accent: Sacramento (script, for special callouts)

### Photography Style
- Natural lighting preferred
- Warm, slightly desaturated tones
- Show real people, real moments — nothing overly staged
- Always include either coffee or flowers (or both) in frame

## Voice & Tone

- Warm and welcoming, never pretentious
- Knowledgeable but approachable
- We say "studio" not "shop" — it reflects our craft approach
- First-person plural ("we") in all brand communications

## Applications

These guidelines apply across [[Social Media Strategy]], [[Email Marketing]], our [[Product Catalog]] photography, and physical signage. All [[Seasonal Campaigns]] materials must be reviewed for brand compliance.

The [[Partnership Opportunities]] document includes co-branding requirements for collaborations.
