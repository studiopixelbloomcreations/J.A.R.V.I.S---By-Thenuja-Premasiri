# Seasonal Campaigns

Seasonal campaigns are the revenue spikes that fund our quieter months. At Bloom & Brew, the intersection of coffee and flowers means we can capitalize on holidays that other businesses can't.

## Annual Campaign Calendar

### Q1
- **Valentine's Day** (our BIGGEST day) — "Love in Bloom" campaign
  - Flower arrangement pre-orders start Jan 15
  - Limited edition rose latte
  - Couples' arrangement workshop
  - Revenue target: 3x average week (see [[Revenue Streams]])

- **Mother's Day** (May, but planning starts Q1) — "For the Woman Who Blooms"
  - Second biggest revenue event
  - Gift bundles: flowers + coffee subscription
  - Brunch pop-up collaboration

### Q2
- **Summer Sipping Series** — Iced coffee flights + summer arrangements
- **Wedding Season** — Push [[Product Catalog]] wedding packages

### Q3
- **Back to School** — Teacher appreciation bundles
- **Fall Launch** — Pumpkin spice + autumn arrangements
- **Small Business Saturday** prep begins

### Q4
- **Holiday Collection** — Wreaths, centerpieces, gift sets
- **Corporate Gifting** — B2B holiday packages (see [[Partnership Opportunities]])
- **New Year** — Subscription launches for [[Loyalty Program]] members

## Execution

Each campaign gets a brief that includes: goals, [[Budget Planning]] allocation, [[Social Media Strategy]] content plan, [[Email Marketing]] sequences, and in-store merchandising. The [[Team Roster]] assigns a campaign lead for each major event.

## Post-Campaign Review

Results are compiled for the [[Quarterly Business Review]] with lessons learned and recommendations for next year.
