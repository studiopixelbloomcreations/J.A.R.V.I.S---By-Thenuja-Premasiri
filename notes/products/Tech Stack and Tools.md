# Tech Stack and Tools

This document catalogs all the technology tools Bloom & Brew uses to run the business. We keep it simple and affordable.

## Current Stack

### Point of Sale
- **Square POS** — Processes all transactions, tracks basic sales data
- Integrated with our [[Loyalty Program]] tracking
- Monthly cost: $0 (free tier + per-transaction fees)

### Communication
- **Slack** (free tier) — Team chat, organized by channels (#coffee, #floral, #general, #scheduling)
- **Google Workspace** — Email (bloomandbrew.com), Drive for document storage, Sheets for tracking
- Used for [[Daily Standup Process]] notes and [[Shift Scheduling]] sheet

### Marketing
- **Mailchimp** (free tier) — [[Email Marketing]] platform
- **Canva** (pro, 1 seat) — Design tool for [[Social Media Strategy]] and [[Brand Guidelines]] assets
- **Later** (free tier) — Social media scheduling
- **Google Business Profile** — Reviews, local SEO, maps

### Finance
- **Wave Accounting** (free) — Bookkeeping, invoicing, [[Budget Planning]] tracking
- **Square Dashboard** — [[Revenue Streams]] daily sales tracking
- **Google Sheets** — Custom financial models, [[Pricing Strategy]] calculators

### Operations
- **Google Sheets** — [[Inventory Management]] tracking, [[Shift Scheduling]]
- **Google Forms** — [[Customer Feedback Program]] surveys, workshop sign-ups
- **Trello** (free) — Project management for [[Seasonal Campaigns]] and [[Growth Roadmap]] initiatives

### Knowledge Management
- **Google Drive** — All documents, [[Training and Onboarding]] materials, [[Quarterly Business Review]] archives
- **Notion** (free) — SOPs, recipes, arrangement guides ([[Product Catalog]] details)
