# Growth Roadmap

This document outlines Bloom & Brew's strategic growth plan for the next 18 months. Our goal is to grow revenue by 40% while maintaining the intimate, community feel that makes us special.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Current - Q1 Next Year)

### Optimize Existing Operations
- Streamline [[Inventory Management]] to reduce waste below 5%
- Implement proper [[Shift Scheduling]] software
- Upgrade [[Tech Stack and Tools]] for better data tracking
- Strengthen [[Supplier Relationships]] with volume commitments

### Grow Digital Presence
- Double [[Social Media Strategy]] following (target: 15K Instagram)
- Grow [[Email Marketing]] list to 6,000 subscribers
- Launch online ordering for arrangements and coffee subscriptions

## Phase 2: Expansion (Q2 - Q3 Next Year)

### Physical Expansion
- Patio build-out for outdoor seating and events (see [[Facilities Maintenance]])
- Add a small event space for private workshops and parties
- [[Budget Planning]] includes $25,000 capital allocation

### Revenue Diversification
- Launch corporate gifting program via [[Partnership Opportunities]]
- Introduce DIY arrangement kits for shipping (new [[Product Catalog]] line)
- Expand workshop offerings (coffee cupping, latte art, wreath making)

## Phase 3: Scale (Q4 Next Year and Beyond)

### Big Moves
- Evaluate second location feasibility
- Franchise model research
- Wholesale private-label coffee program
- E-commerce expansion

## Success Metrics

All tracked in [[Quarterly Business Review]]:
- Revenue growth: 40% over 18 months
- Customer retention: maintain 75%+ via [[Loyalty Program]]
- Net promoter score: 70+ via [[Customer Feedback Program]]
- Team satisfaction: 4.5/5 via [[Employee Wellness Program]] surveys
- [[Revenue Streams]] diversification: no single stream > 30%

## Investment Needed

Detailed in [[Budget Planning]]. Total Phase 1-2 investment: ~$45,000, funded from operations and a small business line of credit.
