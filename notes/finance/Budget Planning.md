# Budget Planning

Bloom & Brew's budget is managed on a quarterly cycle, with monthly check-ins to track actuals against projections.

## Annual Budget Overview

| Category | Annual Budget | % of Revenue |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Cost of Goods (Coffee) | $85,000 | 22% |
| Cost of Goods (Floral) | $62,000 | 16% |
| Payroll & Benefits | $145,000 | 38% |
| Rent & Facilities | $42,000 | 11% |
| Marketing | $18,000 | 5% |
| Technology | $4,800 | 1.2% |
| Insurance & Legal | $8,500 | 2.2% |
| Miscellaneous | $12,000 | 3% |

## Budget Process

1. Department leads submit requests by the 15th of the month before quarter start
2. Owner reviews and adjusts based on [[Revenue Streams]] projections
3. Final budget approved at [[Quarterly Business Review]]
4. Monthly actuals tracked in our accounting spreadsheet (see [[Tech Stack and Tools]])

## Key Budget Items for Next Quarter

## Financial Controls

- All purchases over $200 require owner approval
- [[Inventory Management]] waste is tracked against budget weekly
- [[Supplier Relationships]] terms are renegotiated annually
- Emergency fund: 2 months of operating expenses maintained

## Reporting

Monthly P&L is reviewed by ownership. Quarterly financial summary is presented at [[Quarterly Business Review]] alongside [[Revenue Streams]] performance and [[Growth Roadmap]] investment tracking.
