# Revenue Streams

Bloom & Brew operates multiple revenue streams across our coffee and floral business lines. Diversification is key to weathering seasonal fluctuations.

## Revenue Breakdown (Annual)

| Stream | % of Revenue | Trend |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Coffee (in-store) | 35% | Steady |
| Floral arrangements | 25% | Growing |
| Workshops & events | 15% | Growing fast |
| Coffee subscriptions | 10% | Growing |
| Retail (beans, merchandise) | 8% | Steady |
| Corporate/wholesale | 7% | New, growing |

## Coffee Revenue

Our espresso bar is the daily traffic driver. Average ticket is $6.80, with 180-220 transactions per day. See [[Product Catalog]] for our full menu. [[Pricing Strategy]] covers our margin targets.

## Floral Revenue

Custom arrangements range from $35 (small) to $250+ (event pieces). Wedding season and holidays drive significant spikes — see [[Seasonal Campaigns]] for details. Our [[Partnership Opportunities]] with wedding planners generate high-value orders.

## Workshops

Our floral arrangement workshops ($65-$95/person, 8-12 participants) have become a surprise hit. They drive [[Email Marketing]] signups, [[Social Media Strategy]] content, and [[Loyalty Program]] engagement. Quarterly workshop revenue targets are tracked in [[Budget Planning]].

## Subscriptions

Weekly flower subscriptions ($45/week) and coffee subscriptions ($25/bi-weekly) provide predictable recurring revenue. We have 85 active flower subscribers and 140 coffee subscribers.

## Growth Opportunities

The [[Growth Roadmap]] outlines our plan to grow corporate/wholesale to 15% of revenue through the [[Partnership Opportunities]] pipeline. The [[Quarterly Business Review]] tracks progress against targets.
