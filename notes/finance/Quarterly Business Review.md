# Quarterly Business Review

The Quarterly Business Review (QBR) is our most important strategic meeting at Bloom & Brew. It's where we step back from daily operations and assess the health and direction of the business.

## QBR Agenda Template

### 1. Financial Review (30 min)
- [[Revenue Streams]] performance vs. targets
- [[Budget Planning]] actuals vs. plan
- [[Pricing Strategy]] margin analysis
- Cash flow and runway

### 2. Operations Review (20 min)
- [[Inventory Management]] efficiency and waste metrics
- [[Facilities Maintenance]] updates and needs
- [[Supplier Relationships]] status and any issues
- [[Shift Scheduling]] coverage and labor costs

### 3. Marketing Review (20 min)
- [[Social Media Strategy]] metrics and growth
- [[Email Marketing]] performance
- [[Seasonal Campaigns]] results (if applicable)
- [[Partnership Opportunities]] pipeline update

### 4. Team Review (15 min)
- [[Team Roster]] changes and hiring needs
- [[Training and Onboarding]] progress
- [[Employee Wellness Program]] check-in
- [[Customer Feedback Program]] trends

### 5. Strategic Planning (30 min)
- [[Growth Roadmap]] progress
- [[Loyalty Program]] enrollment and engagement
- [[Product Catalog]] updates
- New initiatives and experiments

### 6. Action Items (15 min)
- Review previous quarter's action items
- Assign new action items with owners and deadlines

## Logistics

- Held on the first Saturday after quarter end
- Studio closed for half day (morning session)
- All [[Team Roster]] leads attend; summary shared with full team
- Catered lunch afterward as a team appreciation moment
