# Pricing Strategy

Our pricing strategy at Bloom & Brew balances premium positioning with neighborhood accessibility. We're not the cheapest, but we deliver exceptional value.

## Pricing Philosophy

- Price reflects quality and experience, not just product cost
- Maintain minimum 65% gross margin on coffee, 55% on floral
- Round to psychologically appealing price points ($4.75 not $4.73)
- Bundle pricing to increase average ticket size

## Coffee Pricing

| Item | Price | Margin |
|------|-------|--------|
| Drip coffee (12oz) | $3.50 | 78% |
| Latte (12oz) | $5.25 | 68% |
| Specialty drink | $6.50-7.50 | 62% |
| Retail beans (12oz bag) | $16.00 | 55% |

## Floral Pricing

| Product | Price Range | Margin |
|---------|------------|--------|
| Small arrangement | $35-55 | 58% |
| Medium arrangement | $65-95 | 55% |
| Large/event | $120-250+ | 52% |
| Workshop fee | $65-95/person | 72% |
| Weekly subscription | $45/week | 50% |

## Competitive Analysis

We survey competitor pricing quarterly. In our area, we're positioned 10-15% above chain coffee shops and comparable to other specialty cafés. Our floral pricing is competitive with local florists but our workshop pricing is premium justified by the experience.
